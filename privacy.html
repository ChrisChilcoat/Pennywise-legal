Pennywise Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

About Pennywise

Pennywise is a personal bookkeeping tool built and run by one individual developer for their own use. It connects to the developer's own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's APIs. It is not offered, sold, or made available to anyone else.

Information Pennywise accesses

When the developer authorizes Pennywise, Intuit grants access to these scopes:

  • QuickBooks Online Accounting (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting): company information and accounting records.
  • QuickBooks Payments (com.intuit.quickbooks.payment): payment and charge records.
  • OpenID Connect profile (openid profile email phone address): the name, email, phone number, and address on the authorizing Intuit account.

How it is used

Data is used only to show and manage the developer's own bookkeeping. Pennywise does not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to that bookkeeping.

Storage and security

  • Pennywise runs on the developer's own computer. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally and are never committed to source control.
  • The app's client secret is loaded from a local environment variable and is never published.
  • QuickBooks data is retrieved on demand and is not copied to any external server or database operated by Pennywise.

Sharing

Pennywise does not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data or personal information with third parties. Data passes only between the developer's computer and Intuit's services.

If Pennywise is later changed to send QuickBooks data to another service, such as an AI model provider, this policy will be updated to name that service before the change is made.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or they expire. To end access at any time, disconnect Pennywise from the QuickBooks company's connected apps settings, or delete the locally stored token file. Either action stops all further data access.

Changes

Any update to this policy is published at this address with a new effective date.

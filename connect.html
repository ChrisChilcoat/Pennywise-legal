Connect or reconnect Pennywise to QuickBooks

Pennywise connects to QuickBooks Online from the developer's own computer using Intuit's OAuth 2.0 sign-in. Reconnecting works the same way as connecting for the first time. Use it if the connection was removed, the tokens expired, or Pennywise reports an authorization error.

  1. On the computer running Pennywise, open a terminal in the Pennywise folder.
  2. Run npm.cmd run auth.
  3. Open http://localhost:8000/connect, sign in to Intuit, and choose the company to connect.

When the page shows "Connected," new tokens have been saved on that computer and you can stop the local server.
